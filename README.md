PharmEasy MedSync+
Smarter Care for Aging Families

MedSync+ is a proposed medication-management experience for PharmEasy,
designed to help elderly and chronic-care patients stay on schedule with
their medicines while enabling trusted family members to support them
remotely.

This project was developed as a product-management case study (PS Sprint
2). It focuses on the problem definition, user personas, feature
prioritization, onboarding experience, product flows, and success
metrics for a medication-adherence solution.
The Problem
Elderly people and patients managing chronic conditions may miss doses
or delay medicine refills because of forgetfulness, complex medication
schedules, digital fatigue, or limited day-to-day support.

These gaps can lead to: - Missed or inconsistent medication intake. -
Medicines running out before a refill is placed. - Last-minute or
emergency orders. - Anxiety for family members who support patients from
another location. - Limited visibility into medication routines and
adherence.

The opportunity identified in the presentation is a unified experience
that anticipates refill needs, sends proactive reminders, and makes it
easier for family members to participate in ongoing medication
management.
Proposed Solution
MedSync+ brings medication tracking, reminders, refill management, and
caregiver coordination into one experience within PharmEasy.

The experience is designed around two connected user groups: -
Patient: manages medicines and daily doses, receives reminders, and
places or schedules refills. - Caregiver: with the patient's
permission, can view medication activity, receive alerts, and help
coordinate refills remotely.

The primary persona prioritized in the case study is Ramesh, 68, an
independent retired adult managing multiple chronic medications.
Priya, 42, his daughter and a software engineer living elsewhere,
represents the caregiver persona.
Key Features
1. Medication management
- Add medicines through prescription upload, past orders, or manual
  entry.
- Capture medicine name, dosage, and timing from an uploaded
  prescription or medicine details.
- View and manage multiple medication schedules.
- Edit or remove medicines and manage refill preferences.
2. Smart reminders and dose tracking
- Configure reminder channels and how early reminders should arrive.
- View a daily "Today's Reminders" list.
- Mark doses as taken and review dose history.
- Track adherence through weekly summaries, pill streaks, and
  missed-versus-taken trends.
3. Refill management
- Monitor remaining medicine inventory and upcoming refill dates.
- Receive refill nudges before expected stockout.
- Reorder an individual medicine or use a "Refill All" action.
- Choose manual or automatic refill, with payment and delivery
  preferences configured accordingly.
- View current orders, delivery estimates, and recent refill history.
4. Family and caregiver coordination
- Invite a trusted family member during onboarding or later.
- Link a caregiver using member details.
- Configure permissions to control what a caregiver can view or do.
- Notify family members when support is needed, including
  refill-related nudges.
- Review linked family members and update access settings.
5. Insights and suggestions
- Review adherence rate, pill streaks, and missed-dose trends.
- View refill timelines and late-refill patterns.
- Receive AI-based suggestions intended to help optimize medication
  adherence.
6. Emergency support
- The proposed experience includes an emergency feature intended to
  alert caregivers and emergency services.
- A cancel option is included to dismiss an accidental trigger.
Emergency-service integration and AI capabilities are proposed product
features in this case study; the presentation does not specify their
technical implementation or operational requirements.

Onboarding Experience
The proposed onboarding flow is designed to make setup manageable for
elderly users while allowing family support where needed.
1. Open the MedSync+ section from the PharmEasy app.
2. Select whether the setup is for the user, a family member, or both.
3. Choose a language and enter relevant member or caregiver details.
4. Invite a trusted family member and configure their permissions.
   Users can skip this step and complete it later.
5. Choose reminder channels and notification timing.
6. Select manual or automatic refill preferences and configure payment
   details where applicable.
7. Add a delivery address.
8. Add medicines by uploading a prescription, selecting past orders, or
   entering details manually.
9. Review the added medicines and proceed to the daily medication
   tracker.
The flow includes progress guidance and "Set Up Later" options to reduce
onboarding friction.

Broader Product Goals
The presentation also identifies four broader goals: - User
retention: encourage useful, ongoing engagement with PharmEasy. -
Feature stickiness: make MedSync+ valuable as part of regular
medication routines. - Family trust: build caregiver confidence
through visibility and coordinated support. - Recurring revenue:
support repeat and scheduled medicine refills.
Product Experience Areas
The proposed experience covers these main areas: - MedSync+ entry point
in the app. - Patient and caregiver onboarding. - Medication setup and
management. - Daily reminders and dose logging. - Inventory monitoring
and refill nudges. - Manual and automatic refill flows. - Order
confirmation and tracking. - Adherence and refill analytics dashboard. -
Family, permission, delivery, payment, and notification settings.
Project Scope and Notes
- Project: PharmEasy MedSync+
- Format: Product-management case study / concept proposal
- Focus: Medication adherence, refill continuity, elderly-friendly
  experience, and remote caregiver coordination
- Primary persona: Elderly chronic-care patient
- Secondary persona: Distant family caregiver
